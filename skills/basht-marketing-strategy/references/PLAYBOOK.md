# Strategy and growth planning
## Strategy hierarchy
Business target → target segment/ICP → positioning and promise → acquisition/retention channels → funnel → experiments → budget guardrails → owners → review cadence. Map B2B diesel logistics separately from B2C flowers and grocery; avoid using retail conversion assumptions for procurement cycles.
## Goals and measurement
Select north-star tied to the business (qualified lead, profitable completed order, repeat purchase). Define baseline/source, target as user-approved assumption, review date and measurement owner. Show unit economics only when COGS, margin, returns and attribution are known; revenue is not profit.
## Experiment backlog
Rank candidate tests using estimated impact, effort and evidence confidence. Each test includes hypothesis, exact change, target segment, KPI, minimum observation window, primary risk and stop condition. Require local constraints: stock, staffing, delivery coverage, seasonality, tax-inclusive prices.
## Deliverable
1-page strategy; 30/60/90-day plan; three budget scenarios explicitly unapproved; experiment register; management decisions; source appendix and data gaps.

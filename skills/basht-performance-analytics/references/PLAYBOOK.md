# Metrics and measurement handbook
## Canonical metrics
CTR=clicks/impressions (percentage when ×100); CPC=spend/clicks; CPM=1000×spend/impressions; CPA=spend/conversions; ROAS=attributed revenue/ad spend; conversion rate=conversions/eligible sessions or clicks (identify denominator); AOV=order revenue/orders. For zero denominator return N/A, never 0 or Infinity. Currency and VAT treatment follow source, not assumed.
## Example — synthetic SAR
Impressions 10,000; clicks 200; spend SAR 400; attributed orders 8; attributed gross sales SAR 1,600. CTR=2%, CPC=SAR 2, CPM=SAR 40, CPA=SAR 50, ROAS=4.0×. Gross margin, refunds and fulfillment unknown: do not call campaign profitable.
## Data quality gates
Check dates/timezones, row coverage, duplicates, campaign identifiers, paused campaigns, missing spend/conversions, currency, conversions attribution window, consent and tracking outages. Report coverage separately from conclusions. A decrease in CTR with a frequency increase is a hypothesis of fatigue, not proof.
## Comparison
Compare equal duration with equivalent weekdays; note promotional and seasonal shifts. Small samples cannot support strong conclusions. Provide attribution caveat and confidence level.
## Deliverable
Source and window; denominator definitions; QA log; metrics table; diagnosed hypotheses; top 3 safe recommendations (not changes).

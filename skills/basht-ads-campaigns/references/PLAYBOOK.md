# Paid-media campaign controls
## Preconditions
Verify account access, legal entity, billing authority, objective, geographic region, product policy, landing page, measurement tag, advertiser identity and approved max budget. Access is not permission to spend. Document approval with campaign/account, platforms, geography, spend cap, schedule, creative and change scope.
## Build sheet
Hierarchy: campaign objective → ad set targeting/placement/optimization/budget → each creative copy and destination/UTM. Ensure campaign/ad set naming conventions, device/language, exclusions, learning-phase changes, frequency and remarketing consent where applicable.
## Budget math
Proposed daily cap = total test budget / days only for an explicitly even allocation; never infer a budget. Break-even CPA ≤ gross contribution margin per newly acquired buyer, only when margin is verified. Report costs inclusive/exclusive of VAT and platform fees as evidenced.
## Measurement
Capture click IDs/UTMs without exposing identities; compare platform-attributed conversions with back-office orders, refunds and cancellations. Invalid or missing tracking blocks ROAS claims, not drafting.
## Approval and rollback
Before action provide exact plan and ask owner. After authorized mutation, read back actual campaign IDs, status, spend limits and creatives; if readback fails, report unknown, do not claim done. No connectors → no creation; simulate a build sheet only.

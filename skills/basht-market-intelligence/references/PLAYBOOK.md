# Market research and competitive intelligence — execution reference
## Sources and provenance
Every claim has source type (official primary, provider first-party, third-party, community, inference), source name/URL or supplied file reference, retrieval date (Asia/Riyadh), observation period, geography, confidence (high/medium/low). Source publication date ≠ price observation date. Cache or screenshots are not current facts. For conflicting prices, display alternatives with timestamp, VAT status and delivery fees instead of silently choosing.
## Research procedure
- Define category boundaries, geography and time window. For grocery: in-store vs delivery app, wholesale vs retail, price unit kg/pack/item, freshness and delivery must be normalized before comparison.
- Identify rivals only from observed evidence; create comparison dimensions: assortment, operating model, price bands, availability, quality signal, trust proof, promotion, channel and delivery area.
- Segment customer jobs-to-be-done and willingness-to-pay as hypotheses, not survey findings unless measured.
- Opportunity score = impact (1–5) × confidence (1–5) / effort (1–5), explicitly a prioritization heuristic, not a predicted ROI.
## Synthetic acceptance scenario
Competitor A sells 1 kg at SAR 12; competitor B sells 500 g at SAR 7. Equivalent price is A SAR 12/kg, B SAR 14/kg; do not ignore VAT/shipping differences, claim representativeness, or invent store names. If no live search exists, return a prepared research plan plus supplied data analysis.
## Deliverable
Business question; coverage; dated source register; comparison table; price normalization; factual findings vs hypotheses; top three experiments; missing evidence and first action.

# Mobile short-video production
## Beat structure
Hook (0–3 s), promise, story/proof, product/action, CTA. Not every creative must follow this rigid formula; optimize for the platform and concept. Deliver timeline with shot seconds that sum to requested duration, visuals, spoken words, subtitles, soundscape and cut transitions.
## Technical checks
Confirm actual frame rate, pixel size, codec, bitrate, loudness, duration, aspect ratio, licensing, text safe areas and Arabic direction on the exported file. ffmpeg installed ≠ video-generation model connected. Never synthesize a successful export.
## Sound and visual QA
No distracting stock sound effects, avoid music overwhelming spoken narration; inspect audible timing and subtitles. For branding-based morphing, preserve provided logo geometry and established colors; if not possible, show an explicit draft storyboard.
## Synthetic acceptance scenario
15-second video must have 15 seconds in timeline, one hook and one CTA; no copyrighted music in mock playback. Distinguish storyboard file from rendered MP4.

# Period-end, tax, audit and reporting operating standard

## Closing checklist
1. Confirm fiscal period/calendar, accounting basis, entity, authorization and locks.
2. Check transaction imports, sales/purchases, voids, returns, cash and bank.
3. Reconcile AR/AP, payroll, tax, stock, inventory, fixed assets and loan accounts to GL.
4. Review accruals/prepayments, unbilled revenue, provisions, FX, intercompany, suspense and unusual accounts.
5. Prepare proposed adjustments and supporting documents; **do not post** without authorized source-system workflow.
6. Re-run TB and reconciliations after approved adjustments; assemble P&L, BS, CF and notes with period comparisons.
7. Owner sign-off; lock/close only via explicitly authorized permitted workflow, with change log.

## Saudi VAT / ZATCA
- Saudi VAT often uses 15% for standard-rated transactions, but rates, effective dates, zero-rating, exemptions, adjustments and applicability must be tested from current official rules for the specific transaction.
- Separate calculation consistency, tax invoice completeness, input VAT deductibility, return reconciliation and e-invoice XML/reporting/clearance verification.
- QR code presence does not prove successful e-invoice submission or regulatory compliance. Never submit or modify ZATCA documents without authorization.
- For any current legal claim cite official ZATCA/SOCPA or relevant authority source; if not available clearly flag pending verification.
- Tax reconciliation: invoice-level output VAT − valid input VAT ± documented adjustments = provisional liability; reconcile to filed return and GL before calling confirmed.
- Protect taxpayer and employee personal identifiers.

## Audit review
- Define assertion: existence, completeness, accuracy, valuation, rights/obligations, cut-off, presentation.
- Test controls and substantive details on a documented population, period, selection method and sample. Report sample scope vs full population explicitly.
- Fraud indicator is not fraud proof; describe unexplained anomalies and recommend escalation.
- Management schedules are not external assurance. No audit opinion, certification, final tax determination or compliance guarantee.

## Report layout
1. Objective, business entity, system, scope, periods, timezone and functional/presentation currency.
2. Source list, retrieval timestamp and controls/capabilities used.
3. Completeness: record coverage; pagination; GL/subledger tie; missing evidence.
4. Executive KPIs with traceable references and calculation methods.
5. Ranked exceptions and root causes (verified versus hypothesized).
6. Proposals, priority and responsible approver.
7. Assumptions/judgment calls; open questions; change log including "no changes" if applicable.
8. Append line-level detail for reconciliations and any aggregate.

## Response policy
- Owner WhatsApp: minimal Arabic lines, clear verified amount and currency, IDs, warning and next action.
- Full report: exact numbers and citations adjacent to each accounting assertion. Avoid generic filler.
- Never reuse saved user history as proof of current ERP balances.
- For drafts of adjustments, show account/date/debit/credit/period/evidence and whether unposted.

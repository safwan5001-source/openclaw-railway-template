# Accounting procedures and calculation controls

## Bookkeeping and trial balance
- Check each journal for debit and credit equality by currency and functional-currency equivalent, posting period, authorization, account activity, dimension validity and double posting.
- Obtain GL opening + movements = GL closing by account. Sum debit balances vs credit balances for trial balance; differences must be investigated.
- Trial balance arithmetical agreement does **not** prove occurrence, valuation, completeness, authorization or appropriate classification.
- Reversals and accruals require evidence and correct periods; preserve closed periods by default.

## Bank reconciliation
- Match original bank statement lines to book cash/bank ledger including fees, reversals, charges, cash-in-transit, uncleared payments and bulk settlements.
- Bank reconciliation: adjusted bank balance = bank statement ending balance + deposits in transit − outstanding payments ± bank errors; adjusted book balance = GL balance + verified book adjustments. Equal adjusted balances are necessary but not sufficient.
- Never add unverified deposits as booked revenue. Identify unmatched and duplicated refs and disclose date-window coverage.

## AR / AP and payments
- Opening party balance + posted invoices/debits − credits − settled receipts = closing AR, subject to FX/write-off/reclassification adjustments.
- Investigate partial receipts, split allocations, advances, credit notes, refunds, cancellations, retention and timing. Allocation sum may not exceed verified payment.
- Aging uses open invoice balances and a clearly stated as-of date; drafts and voids excluded from posted receivables.
- AP tie: opening payable + accepted purchases/expenses − supplier credits − posted payments ± adjustments = closing; match PO, receipt and invoice when available.
- A matching invoice total and receipts list is not proof a ledger is correct; tie GL control accounts.

## Revenue and VAT
- Verify quantity × price, discount, tax-inclusive/exclusive treatment, tax base, rounding by line vs document as configured, and final total.
- Tax-exclusive: gross = taxable base + VAT. Tax-inclusive (where applicable): taxable base = gross / (1 + rate), VAT = gross − base, with source-prescribed rounding.
- Assess applicable VAT rate and exemption for date/jurisdiction/product; never assume a universal tax rate or issue legal sign-off.
- Revenue recognition requires performance and contract evidence, not invoice date alone.

## COGS, stock and gross margin
- Closing stock = opening quantity + receipts − issues ± adjustments (after UoM conversion).
- COGS = opening inventory + net purchases + qualifying acquisition costs − closing inventory when appropriate; otherwise use validated perpetual movements.
- Gross profit = net recognized revenue − related COGS; gross margin % = gross profit / net revenue ×100 when denominator nonzero.
- Show quantity vs valuation variance, damaged/expired inventory, returns, movement cut-off and stock-count reconciliations.

## Assets / depreciation
- Asset cost = recognized purchase + directly attributable costs per policy; exclude expenses not qualifying for capitalization.
- Straight-line depreciation per period = (depreciable cost − residual value) / useful life with proration policy. Reconcile register to accumulated depreciation and disposal postings.
- Do not invent useful lives or capitalization thresholds.

## Payroll
- Source authorized payroll register, attendance/contract where relevant, bank transfer and liability control accounts. Reconcile gross pay − documented deductions = net pay, plus separately specified employer charges.
- Treat statutory deductions, leave accrual, end-of-service and jurisdictions as policy- and date-specific.

## Financial statements and cash flow
- Profit = recognized revenues − relevant costs/expenses; do not call sales less bank payments profit.
- Balance sheet: assets = liabilities + equity after accounting adjustments and classifications.
- Cash flow from actual cash movements, appropriately classified into operating/investing/financing; indirect method reconciles profit to net cash with working capital and noncash adjustments.
- Budget variance = actual − budget; state positive/negative interpretation for cost/revenue KPIs.
- Forecasts must present assumptions, ranges, drivers and scenario sensitivity; never blend projections into historical actuals.

## Materiality and exception priority
Rank by possible misstatement, fraud indicators, time sensitivity, recurrence and monetary exposure; avoid inventing a numeric materiality threshold.
For every exception: source evidence → expected rule → observed value → difference → impact → proposed remediation → accountable reviewer → unresolved dependency.

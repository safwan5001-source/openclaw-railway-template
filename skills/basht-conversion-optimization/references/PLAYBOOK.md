# Conversion diagnostics and experimentation
## Funnel
Segment ad click → landing → product view → add to cart → checkout → payment → fulfilled order; for local grocery, walk-ins and phone/WhatsApp leads can be separate conversion paths.
## Audit
Evidence from observed screens, legitimate analytics and permitted files only. Prioritize mobile navigation, clear pricing (VAT/shipping), inventory, service coverage, contact CTA, checkout friction and failed form states. Do not call an unmeasured drop-off factual.
## Testing
Define a single primary metric and guardrails (refunds, contribution margin, customer satisfaction). Use proper random assignment and adequate sample before claiming statistical uplift; otherwise label usability heuristic. Avoid dark patterns, countdown fraud and deceptive scarcity.
## Example
100 orders from 5,000 sessions = 2% session conversion. 110 from 5,000 = 2.2%, a 0.2 percentage-point and 10% relative increase; cannot claim significance from those aggregate counts alone.
## Deliverable
Evidence-backed issue queue, hypothesis, variant, metric, counter-metric, priority, effort, QA and approval gate.

# Creative and brand QA
## Brand source of truth
Resolve the latest approved identity and owner-supplied assets before drafting. Distinguish source assets from AI mockups. Do not alter original marks without permission. Proofread Arabic manually for hamza, hamza on alif, tā marbūṭa, punctuation, brand name spelling, and offer arithmetic.
## Creative matrix
Concept = audience tension × single value proposition × proof × channel. Deliver at least two materially distinct concepts, not color variants. For each: headline (short and legible on mobile), supporting text, CTA, required visual, claim evidence, brand mapping and measurable objective.
## QA gates
No misrepresented actual store photos, discounts, prices, testimonials, stock or guarantees. Check safe area per actual platform specs, contrast and accessibility, right-to-left typography, image rights and consent. For image generation, verify output actually exists and inspect text; generative text defects trigger correction, not approval.
## Deliverable
Brief, concept matrix, production shots/assets, proof checklist, QA status PASS/FAIL/UNVERIFIED; no publish.

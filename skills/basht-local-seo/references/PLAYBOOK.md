# Local SEO and store profile review
## Audit areas
NAP consistency, official location and categories, listed hours, actual opening status, website mobile performance, crawlability/indexability, local landing page intent, delivery area, reviews and duplicate listings. Verify all location data with source, do not fabricate branch addresses.
## Search intent
Map Arabic local queries to transactional, navigational and informational intent with distinct pages. Provide bilingual titles/descriptions only where warranted, and avoid keyword stuffing or fake review tactics.
## Measurement
Search Console impressions/clicks/CTR/rank and Google Business Profile calls/directions/website visits are distinct signals; do not promise a ranking. No official tool → suggest manual read-only verification or mark unverified.
## Deliverable
Issues with observed vs assumed distinction, severity, evidence URL/screenshot/date, recommended change, dependency, success metric and approval state.
